## Overview

Greater Hyderabad Municipal Corporation (GHMC) invites e-procurement bids to appoint a consultant for surveying, digitization and geo-referencing of its immovable assets (parks, markets, shops, buildings, lands and similar) for LB Nagar Zone and Kukatpally Zone, under Notice No. RFP-01/TP/HO/GHMC/2025-26. Selection is by QCBS and the contract period is 6 Months. [pp. 1, 5]

## Issuing organization

Greater Hyderabad Municipal Corporation (GHMC); O/o Chief City Planner, Town Planning Section, Head Office, Tank Bund, Hyderabad; Notice No. RFP-01/TP/HO/GHMC/2025-26 dt: 09.04.2025; ccp.ghmc@gmail.com, 8897723423 [pp. 1, 5]

## Scope

- Detailed physical survey of GHMC immovable properties using DGPS to collect boundary measurements, extent, physical features, building foot prints and surrounding features. [pp. 21, 22]
- Collect real world co-ordinates of every property corner using DGPS, and record height above MSL for each property. [pp. 22, 23]
- Prepare detailed plans of each property at a scale of 1 : 200 to 1:1000 and capture photographs of frontage, sides and inside. [p. 22]
- Collect property attribute details (circle, layout, extent, Sy. No., encroachment, boundaries, GPS coordinates and others) and generate a Unique ID Number for each property. [p. 22]
- Design and develop a geo-enabled mobile application integrated with base map/Google Map for survey and attribute collection, and integrate attribute data with spatial data. [p. 22]
- Prepare a profile of every immovable property and the Inventory of immovable properties in the prescribed format. [p. 22]
- Develop and implement a software application to monitor the immovable properties of GHMC. [pp. 22, 26]
- Deliverables include raw and corrected DGPS readings in RINEX and native format, maps in CAD and KML format with shape files, and the report in Hard Copy (8 Sets) plus soft copy. [p. 25]

## Key requirements

- Each Pre-Qualification criterion in Section 4.1 is mandatory; a bid that fails any one is non-responsive and not evaluated further. [p. 10]
- DGPS rover readings at each property corner must have a minimum observation period of 15 minutes; RTK-DGPS is preferred. [p. 22]
- Base stations must use Multi/Dual frequency DGPS receivers with a minimum observation time of 12 hours from the nearest SOI control point. [p. 23]
- Base-to-rover distance must be less than 10 km for single frequency DGPS rovers and less than 50 kms for dual frequency rovers. [p. 23]
- DGPS instruments must be set to Geographic Coordinate System WGS84 Datum. [p. 23]
- Raw and differentially corrected DGPS readings with error charts must be submitted to the Authority every week or as soon as the work is completed, whichever is earlier. [p. 23]
- Survey is proposed in all 6 zones simultaneously with at least 2 Teams per Zone; each Team has a Team Leader (Specialist in Geomatics), a DGPS Operator and/or ATS Operator, and surveyors. [p. 24]
- Key personnel minimum qualifications are prescribed; the Project Manager needs B.E/B.Tech or equivalent and at least 5 years of experience, and CVs of key personnel must be enclosed. [pp. 24, 25]
- Coordinates must be delivered in 8 decimal places in degrees for Latitude and Longitude including Height above MSL in meters. [p. 25]
- Source Code of the customized GIS Solution must be transferred to GHMC; software licences must be genuine, perpetual and in the name of GHMC. [pp. 28, 29]
