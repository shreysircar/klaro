## Overview

Westchester Medical Center Health Network (WMCHealth) issued RFP # WF41CBF seeking qualified firms to provide Enterprise Technical Services and Epic Implementation Support Services across its Hudson Valley, New York hospitals. Technical Services span application management, service desks, desktop, infrastructure, network, security and service management; Epic services cover planning, build, activation and post-go-live optimization, with go-live targeted for End of 2027. The agreement runs (1) year and automatically renews for four (4) one (1) year periods. [pp. 3, 10, 15]

## Issuing organization

Westchester Medical Center Health Network; contracting parties: Westchester County Health Care Corporation d/b/a Westchester Medical Center, Bon Secours Charity Health System, Inc., HealthAlliance of the Hudson Valley; RFP # WF41CBF; contact: Georgiann Coloccia, Network Director, IT Operations [pp. 1, 5, 13]

## Scope

- Technical Services for Westchester Medical Center (Valhalla), MidHudson Regional Hospital, Bon Secours Charity Health System (Good Samaritan, Bon Secours Community, St. Anthony's) and HealthAlliance Hospital. [p. 8]
- Service towers: Application Management Services (AMS), AMS Enhancement Services, Technical Service Desk, Clinical Service Desk, Desktop & Digital Workplace, Infrastructure & Network, Security, and Service Management & Governance. [p. 9]
- Infrastructure & Network (Tier 2 & Tier 3) support: server, storage and database support, network operations and monitoring, and disaster recovery coordination. [p. 9]
- Security and service management: identity and access management, Privileged Access Management (PAM), Identity Governance and Administration (IGA), ServiceNow, SailPoint and LogicMonitor tooling, ITIL-aligned processes and KPI dashboards. [pp. 9, 10]
- Epic Implementation Services: Epic-certified leadership and analysts, workflow design, integrated testing, interface and reporting build, data conversion planning, training coordination and at-the-elbow support planning. [p. 10]
- Epic go-live readiness for a Big Bang activation: Technical Dress Rehearsal (TDR), At-The-Elbow (ATE) surge staffing and post-implementation stabilization and optimization. [p. 11]
- Volumes include 10,000 desktops/laptops, 5,000 mobile devices, 17,000 users and UCC mailboxes, TSD Helpdesk 11,000, 925 Windows and 176 Linux servers, 2,800 TB storage, 3,300 network devices and 19,300 Active Directory. [pp. 11, 12]
- Attachment H assigns the vendor transition planning, L2/L3 incident and problem management, SailPoint ISC and CyberArk PAM implementation and managed services, and Active Directory administration. [pp. 31, 34, 35]
