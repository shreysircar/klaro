## Overview

National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development (NABARD), Department of Information Technology, Mumbai, invites e-bids for supply of Red Hat JBoss Enterprise Application Platform (EAP) and Extended Life Support (ELS) subscriptions for 1 year, to upgrade from JBoss 5, which went out of support in 2019. Bids are submitted online in two parts, Technical and Commercial. Tender Reference No. NB. HO. DIT/91165/DIT-14-20 dated 22nd September 2025. [pp. 3, 4]

## Issuing organization

National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development (NABARD); Department of Information Technology, Bandra Kurla Complex, Mumbai - 400051; Tender Reference No. NB. HO. DIT/91165/DIT-14-20 dated 22nd September 2025; contact Shri Anuj Bagaria, Assistant Manager [pp. 1, 4, 6]

## Scope

- Supply Red Hat JBoss EAP and EAP Extended Life Support (ELS) support subscriptions for one year, and provide Red Hat support services as and when required under the subscribed plan. [p. 10]
- Quantity is 2 of each of four products: EAP 16-Core Premium (MW0153748), EAP ELS Program 16-Core Premium (MW2132048), EAP 16-Core Standard (MW0196814), EAP ELS Program 16-Core Standard (MW2122821). [p. 11]
- Supply the subscriptions within 48 hours from the date of issue of the contract or purchase order. [p. 10]
- Provide access to Red Hat support services, including upgrades, updates, patches and technical assistance as per the subscribed plan. [p. 11]
- Assist in activation and configuration of JBoss EAP and ELS support on the Bank's infrastructure, and provide documentation and support for compliance and auditing. [p. 11]
- Coordinate with the Red Hat support team to resolve issues raised by the Bank, and give timely renewal notifications and assistance for subscription continuity. [p. 11]
- Deliverables: valid support subscription, access credentials and documentation for Red Hat support services, technical support and issue resolution, and compliance and audit documentation. [p. 11]
- Provide contact details for support and escalation; support requests are handled under the SLA defined in the subscription plan. [p. 12]
