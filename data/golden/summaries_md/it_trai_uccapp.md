## Overview

Telecom Regulatory Authority of India (TRAI) invites bids, limited to its Empaneled Bidders, for system study, design, development, testing and implementation of a Mobile App for managing Unsolicited Commercial Communication (Android and iOS) and a Reporting Dashboard/Portal, hosted on NIC Cloud in New Delhi. The contract covers one (01) year of free warranty support after Go-live followed by two (02) years of maintenance. Ref. No. IT-6/1/4(1)/2021-IT. [pp. 4, 7, 11]

## Issuing organization

Telecom Regulatory Authority of India (TRAI); Technical Officer (IT); Ref. No. IT-6/1/4(1)/2021-IT; Mahanagar Doorsanchar Bhawan, J.L. Nehru Marg (Old Minto Road), New Delhi - 110002; bid queries: it-eoirfp@trai.gov.in [pp. 1, 4, 5, 6]

## Scope

- Design, develop, test and implement a Mobile App for managing Unsolicited Commercial Communication for consumers, on both Android and iOS platforms. [pp. 7, 19]
- Develop a Reporting Portal: TRAI login with filter-based reports downloadable as csv, excel or pdf, and published interactive reports for consumers. [p. 23]
- Build an interactive UCC dashboard with analytics and reports such as total complaints, complaint status, TSP-wise, LSA-wise, complaint category and header reports. [p. 19]
- App features include OTP validation through the TSP, DND preference setting as per schedule II of TCCCPR, 2018, UCC SMS/call complaints, consent revocation, appeals and header query. [p. 22]
- End-to-end API integration and API testing of the App and Portal with TSP platforms, plus bug fixing support to TSPs; Annexure E gives the indicative API set. [pp. 19, 23]
- Deploy, host and monitor the App and Portal on NIC Cloud virtual machines provided by TRAI, optimizing resource utilization. [pp. 8, 20]
- Maintenance for two (02) years after the warranty period: bug fixing, patches, quarterly app updates, backups, monitoring and quarterly status reports, as per Annexure B. [pp. 12, 24]
- Exhaustive training to TRAI officials, TSPs, Appellate Authority and other stakeholders, with e-learning videos, PowerPoint and PDF training material. [p. 8]
