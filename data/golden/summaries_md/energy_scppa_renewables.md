## Overview

Southern California Public Power Authority (SCPPA), on behalf of its Member utilities, solicits proposals for renewable energy projects or products, optionally with co-located energy storage, for commercial operation or delivery starting in 2025 and beyond. Structures may be SCPPA ownership or a power purchase agreement with or without an ownership option. Rolling RFP issued February 14, 2025; proposals due June 30, 2025. [p. 1]

## Issuing organization

Southern California Public Power Authority (SCPPA), acting for its Member utilities; Q1/Q2 2025 Renewable Energy Resources and Energy Storage Solutions RFP; contact renewablesrfp@scppa.org; reference number not specified in the document [pp. 1, 13]

## Scope

- Renewable energy projects or products under the California RPS programs, with commercial operation or delivery starting in 2025 and beyond. [p. 1]
- Accepted structures: SCPPA project ownership, a PPA with an ownership option, or a PPA without an ownership option. [p. 1]
- Eligible resources include wind, geothermal, biomass, biomethane, hydro, solar, solar thermal, waste-to-energy, renewable fuel cells, permanent load shifting, hydrogen, flexible and hybrid projects, and unbundled RECs. [pp. 5, 6]
- Energy storage may be proposed only combined with a renewable energy project. [pp. 1, 6]
- Portfolio Content Category 2 and 3 REC-only proposals are also considered, for any term. [p. 4]
- Delivery term: minimum is 1 month with no maximum; both short-term and long-term (greater than 10 years) delivery sought. [p. 7]
- Delivery at the listed points of delivery (CAISO Grid with preference of SP15, among others) or other points if all transmission, ancillary and scheduling costs are included. [p. 8]
- Out of scope: stand-alone energy storage, to be proposed under SCPPA's separate Standalone Energy Storage RFP. [pp. 1, 6]

## Key requirements

- Proposal must be signed by an officer authorized to bind the Respondent, with the non-collusion declaration in the transmittal letter. [pp. 4, 5]
- Only proven renewable technologies; experimental or speculative proposals will not be entertained. [p. 2]
- Facility must be RPS compliant and compliant with the California Energy Commission emissions performance standard (EPS) upon COD and throughout the term. [p. 3]
- Seller must assume the risk of bringing the facility back into RPS or EPS compliance if a change in law makes it noncompliant. [p. 3]
- Energy storage paired with a renewable project must have capacity of at least one half the total interconnection capacity offered and a duration of at least 4 hours. [p. 6]
- Respondent must handle site control (SCPPA must be allowed to assume or cure any default in site control documents) and bears environmental liability, hazardous materials and removal. [p. 3]
- Respondent must obtain all construction, operational and environmental permits and licenses, and construction financing. [p. 4]
- SCPPA must receive all environmental attributes and all capacity rights associated with the project. [pp. 3, 9]
- Project must be certified RPS-eligible by the California Energy Commission no later than 6 months after COD and registered in WREGIS no later than 3 months before COD. [p. 11]
- If a SCPPA Member is in the EIM, acceptance into the Energy Imbalance Market is required before any test energy; Seller must give technical design data at least nine months before test energy. [p. 10]

## Commercial requirements

- Fixed energy price (preferred): nominal $/MWh as of the year of COD, no escalation, covering energy, capacity, RECs and all other products. [p. 7]
- Alternative: variable index energy price plus fixed capacity/REC price in nominal $/MWh with no escalation; preferred by some Member utilities. [p. 7]
- Storage price must be quoted in addition to the energy price, in $/MWh and in $/kW-month for a minimum of 4-hour systems; include a Solar-Only price if solar and BESS are proposed. [p. 7]
- Any price increase after proposal submittal could end negotiations and prompt reevaluation of the proposal. [p. 7]
- Transmission cost to the delivery point must be in the energy price; CAISO delivery must include a $/MWh price adder fixed for the contract term. [pp. 8, 9]
- Ownership offer must state a purchase price at COD in $/kW plus recurring owner costs; PPA with purchase option must give a buyout price or formula and an energy price without buyout. [pp. 9, 10]
- Performance Assurance amounts in nominal dollars must be stated, through COD and after COD as a Letter of Credit; SCPPA may require performance security before negotiations. [pp. 11, 16]
- Not specified in the document: EMD or bid security, tender fee, payment terms, liquidated damages and warranty
