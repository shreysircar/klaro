## Overview

Solar Energy Corporation of India Limited (SECI), on behalf of MNRE, invites proposals from an agency to establish a Programme Management Unit supporting the National Mission on Cleantech Manufacturing. The engagement is for an initial period of two (2) years, extendable by one (1) year at the discretion of SECI/MNRE. Ref. SECI/C&P/MI/00/05/26-27 dated 15.09.2026. [pp. 1, 4]

## Scope

- Establish a PMU to support MNRE on the National Mission on Cleantech Manufacturing across solar, wind, green hydrogen and energy storage, plus other cleantech manufacturing sectors such as EVs and advanced batteries, very high voltage transmission equipment and waste to energy. [pp. 4, 7]
- Work is structured as two integrated work packages: strategy, indigenization and ecosystem (Work Package 1) and states strategy, investment mobilization and implementation (Work Package 2). [pp. 10, 12]
- Work Package 1: value chain reports for solar, wind, green hydrogen and energy storage; benchmarking against at least five peer economies; sector indigenization strategies with year-wise localization targets till 2030. [pp. 10, 11]
- Work Package 1 also covers policy and investment frameworks, editable sector financial models, R&D and skilling roadmaps, and stakeholder consultations (at least 10 per sector). [pp. 11, 12]
- Work Package 2: state-wise manufacturing upscaling strategy identifying 15 States/UTs, manufacturing cluster facilitation plans, investment pipeline tracker, circular economy and raw material strategy, and state ease-of-doing-business support. [pp. 12, 13]
- Work Package 2 also covers a KPI-linked results framework, a consolidated report on cleantech manufacturing in India, state handholding, and any other related assignments directed by MNRE. [p. 13]
- Additional scope: monthly price trend and forecasting reports, carbon credit pricing and market intelligence module, policy updates, quarterly deep-dive assessments, ad-hoc alerts within 72 hours and an annual outlook with three-year projections. [pp. 14, 15]
- Agency may also be required to provide back-end support as directed by MNRE. [p. 9]
