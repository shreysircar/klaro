## Overview

New Delhi Municipal Council (NDMC), through its Medical Officer of Health, seeks an Independent Consultant to review, monitor and certify the work of the Concessionaire on its 8-year PPP project for collection and transportation of municipal solid waste across the NDMC area. The consultant's term is five years, with extension considered on performance. Selection is by Quality and Cost Based evaluation of technical and financial bids submitted through the e-procurement portal. [pp. 4, 5, 14]

## Issuing organization

New Delhi Municipal Council (NDMC); Office of the Medical Officer of Health; tender/reference number not specified in the document; contact: Medical Officer of Health, Room No. 1002, 10th Floor, Palika Kendra, NDMC, New Delhi-110001 [pp. 1, 4, 6]

## Scope

- Act as Independent Consultant to review designs and drawings and monitor and certify the Concessionaire's collection and transportation of MSW in the NDMC area, per Schedule E of the Concession Document. [p. 4]
- Project area served by the Concessionaire: approximately 2.94 lakhs population across 14 circles of NDMC. [p. 4]
- Independently review, monitor and where required approve design, construction, operation and maintenance of Project Facilities, and review safety and environment management measures. [p. 18]
- Implementation Period: review the Concessionaire's Transition Plan, Operations Plan and monthly progress reports, verify compliance with Design and Construction Requirements and MSW Rules, and issue the Readiness Certificate. [pp. 18, 19]
- Operations Period: verify weighment, MSW testing and weighbridge and Waste Inspection Area records at Treatment and Landfill Facilities, and ensure construction debris is not mixed with MSW. [p. 19]
- Monthly random inspections of Garbage Stations, Street Corner Bins, transportation and loading vehicles, Transfer Station and door to door collection. [p. 20]
- Maintain records, issue monthly inspection reports and Notices to Remedy, report Material and Persistent Breach of O&M Requirements, and review the Awareness Campaign. [pp. 21, 22]
- At the end of the Concession Period, monitor handback and transfer of Project Facilities and issue a certificate of compliance; also certify adequacy of insurance and verify termination payments. [pp. 20, 53]
