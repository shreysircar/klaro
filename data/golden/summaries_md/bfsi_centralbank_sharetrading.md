## Overview

Central Bank of India seeks share broking houses to offer an online share trading platform and Demat support services to its customers under its 3-in-1 scheme (savings account, Demat account, trading account). Contract is 3 years from go-live, extendable. There should be no cost to the Bank; bidders quote a revenue share. [pp. 1, 18, 41]

## Issuing organization

Central Bank of India; Resources Department, 4th Floor, Chander Mukhi, Nariman Point, Mumbai-400001; RFP Ref No. GEM/2025/B/6828132 dated 27/10/2025; bids to Deputy General Manager, Resources Department, 1st Floor, Bajaj Bhavan, Nariman Point, Mumbai 400021; Tel 022-66387502/7780 [pp. 1, 6]

## Scope

- Open the customer's Demat account with the Bank and trading account with the broker in one seamless digital journey on the Bank's CenteeZ app and other digital channels; preferable turnaround is 10-15 minutes. [p. 8]
- Provide a web and mobile online trading platform with real-time market data, market, limit and stop-loss orders, call and trade, reports, and trading in NSE and BSE secondary market, IPO, Govt securities, mutual funds, futures and options, sovereign gold bonds, currency, REITs and fixed income. [pp. 8, 9, 36]
- Integrate the platform with the Bank's core banking, DPSecure back office and CDSL/NSDL systems, with fund transfer and lien marking and unmarking on Demat holdings; back-office integration is at the bidder's cost. [pp. 36, 37]
- Provide Demat support services: grievance redressal, control register, register of investor customers, MIS reports, and a dedicated website with complaint tracking including SEBI SCORES. [pp. 9, 37]
- Promote the 3-in-1 scheme across the country and train Bank staff at Central Office, Zonal Offices, Regional Offices and branches; give on-site handholding to corporate customers who opt in. [p. 37]
- Award is not exclusive: the Bank already has a tie-up with Motilal Oswal Financial Services Ltd (MOFSL) and plans one or more additional partners. [pp. 8, 36]
- Platform should also support loan against shares, BTST, algorithmic trading and a pre-built basket of stocks and ETFs; Margin Trading Facility (MTF) may be provided, with risks borne by the bidder. [pp. 39, 40]
- Provide reverse transition and seamless migration to the Bank or a new bidder on exit, including training and documentation; its cost must be part of the commercial offer. [pp. 13, 38]

## Key requirements

- Bidder must comply with all points mentioned in the Scope of work, although many are worded 'should'; non-compliance of any point will lead to rejection of the bid. [p. 11]

## Eligibility

- Bidder must be a legal entity in India, preferably a Public/Private limited company majority owned by Indian residents; Government organisations, PSE/PSU and start-ups meeting all other criteria are also eligible. [p. 14]
- Bidder must be in broking business with valid SEBI registration and valid NSE and BSE membership, and have minimum 5 years of experience in broking business operations. [p. 14]
- Bidder must have positive net worth and positive net profit in the last three financial years (FY 2022-23, FY 23-24 & FY 24-25), shown by audited statements and a Chartered Accountant net worth certificate; provisional statements certified by a Chartered Accountant are accepted for FY 24-25 if audited results are unavailable. [p. 14]
- Bidder must have Pan India experience with retail clients and be tied up with at least one scheduled commercial bank for trading services; this does not apply to Government/PSE/PSU bidders. [pp. 14, 15]
- Bidder must not be an associate or subsidiary of a banking company or vice versa, and must be non-bank promoted: not promoted by competitor banks or their controlling groups, and no competitor bank holding equity of 10% and above or having a Board representative. [p. 15]
- There must be no suspension order against the bidder by SEBI, NSDL, CDSL, NSE or BSE in the past three years, and no restraint order pending at any judicial forum in India or abroad on broking business. [p. 15]
- Bidder must have a trading platform approved by NSDL and CDSL with all transactions settled in e-mode, supported by an undertaking. [p. 15]
- A bidder from a country sharing a land border with India is eligible only if registered with the Competent Authority. [p. 27]
