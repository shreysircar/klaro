## Overview

IIM Tiruchirappalli invites sealed proposals (RFP No. 24SPT291T dated 08.01.2025) to select a branding agency. The agency will define the brand purpose, revisit the logo for the digital era, and produce a comprehensive branding guidelines document with designs, artworks and an implementation plan, across English, Hindi and Tamil and sub-brands. Part A is estimated at Rs. 11,00,000/- excluding GST, to be completed within 90 days of the Work Order. [pp. 1, 3, 18]

## Issuing organization

Indian Institute of Management Tiruchirappalli (IIM Tiruchirappalli), Ministry of Education, Government of India; RFP No. 24SPT291T dated 08.01.2025; Chief Administrative Officer (i/c), Trichy - Pudukkottai Main Road, Tiruchirappalli-620 024; purchase@iimtrichy.ac.in [p. 1]

## Scope

- Define the brand purpose: stakeholder discussions, competitor and industry analysis, feedback from students, faculty and alumni, articulation of purpose, mission, vision and values, and target-audience personas. [p. 4]
- Revisit the logo for the digital era: contemporary design appealing to millennial and GenZ audiences, versatile across digital platforms in colour and monochrome, keeping the original logo's essence. [pp. 3, 4, 5]
- Comprehensive branding guidelines document and digital Brand Identity Manual: colour palette, typography, iconography, imagery, logo usage rules, graphic elements, and do's and don'ts, covering sub-brands such as Chennai Campus, Placements and EEC. [pp. 3, 5, 7]
- Signage designs and artworks for Trichy Campus and Chennai Campus in bilingual (Hindi, English) and trilingual (Tamil, Hindi, English) versions, for classrooms, offices, auditorium, entrances, sports block and other locations. [pp. 5, 6]
- Stationery designs for Trichy Campus and Chennai Centre: business cards, identity cards, letterheads (A4, A5, Legal), note sheets, envelopes, visitor passes, corporate gift boxes and memento packaging. [p. 6]
- Advertising and promotion templates: print, TV logo closure, BTL, OOH and event materials, social media graphics and post templates, an IIM Tiruchirappalli hashtag, merchandise, brochures and video guidelines. [p. 6]
- Web page guidelines for the Institute's website: layout, font sizes, line heights, spacing, colour usage, accessibility, cross-device performance and standards for images, icons and multimedia. [pp. 7, 8]
- Implementation plan for introducing the new brand identity internally and externally, which may include training stakeholders on brand usage. [p. 6]

## Key requirements

- The bidder must not sub-let or subcontract any part of the contract to another agency or individual. [pp. 18, 20]
- Branding guidelines must specify preferred materials/make and suitable substitutes for applications such as sign boards, office interiors, furniture, painting, wallpapers, vinyl, printing and paper type. [p. 5]
- A well-defined colour palette must be developed; it should adhere to web accessibility standards and guide colour combinations in different contexts. [p. 7]
- Before starting branding work, the successful bidder should answer the prerequisite brand questions (journey in less than 500 words, 3 key messages, strengths, benefits, desired perception) with IIM stakeholders. [p. 4]
- As the first stage, the bidder is required to conduct a study with IIM Tiruchirappalli to finalize requirements and submit a phased work plan and delivery schedule for approval. [p. 18]
- The Brand Identity guidelines deliverables should be crafted as interactive web pages hosted on the Institute's official website. [p. 6]
- The successful bidder will be required to collaborate with the separately contracted website development and branding teams so the website aligns with the approved branding. [p. 20]
- The bidder must warrant that deliverables do not infringe third-party trademark, copyright, patent or trade secret rights, obtain licences where needed, and indemnify IIM Tiruchirappalli against infringement liability. [pp. 18, 19]
- IIM Tiruchirappalli will own the rights to the branding work on completion, with full rights to use, modify and distribute the materials without limitation. [p. 19]
- The vendor should protect the Institute's proprietary and confidential information; failure will lead to legal action. [p. 19]
