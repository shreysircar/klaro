## Scope

- Culture assessment: conduct a comprehensive culture diagnostic across the merged entities, identifying cultural similarities, differences, conflicts, staff readiness for change and barriers to integration. [p. 51]
- Vision, values and culture design: facilitate leadership workshops to define the desired culture and develop core values and behavioural standards aligned with KDC's strategic objectives. [p. 52]
- Transformation strategy: develop a culture transformation strategy and implementation roadmap, with key initiatives, interventions, quick wins and mechanisms to reinforce desired behaviours. [p. 52]
- Leadership and staff engagement: facilitate leadership alignment and coaching sessions, run staff engagement forums and workshops, and guide leaders on sustaining culture change. [p. 52]
- Communication and change management: develop a communication strategy and design tools, messaging and campaigns to reinforce the desired culture. [p. 52]
- Monitoring, evaluation and sustainability: develop KPIs, monitoring tools and feedback mechanisms, and recommend strategies to institutionalize the new culture. [p. 52]
- Report deliverables: Inception Report, Organizational Culture Assessment Report, Organizational Culture Framework, Culture Transformation Strategy & Implementation Roadmap, Communication and Change Management Plan, Mid-Term Review Report (Year 1), Final Report with sustainability plan. [p. 52]
- Deliver training and capacity-building sessions for internal champions, the leadership team and all other staff. [p. 52]
