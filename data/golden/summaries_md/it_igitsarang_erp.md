## Overview

Indira Gandhi Institute of Technology (IGIT), Sarang, Dhenkanal, Odisha invites proposals for a cloud based, SaaS mode ERP system covering academic, finance, HR, hostel and related modules, plus website re-design. The selected bidder must develop and implement it, then maintain it under a three year contract. The notice is dated 15.04.2021. [pp. 4, 7, 16]

## Issuing organization

Indira Gandhi Institute of Technology (IGIT), Sarang; Sarang, Dhenkanal-759146, Odisha; notice dated 15.04.2021, notice number left blank; bids addressed to The Director [pp. 4, 6]

## Scope

- Install and commission a cloud based SAAS mode ERP; the bidder must develop, test, install and turn over the software to IGIT Sarang, with prices all-inclusive. [p. 7]
- Admission management, academic management (courses, syllabus), attendance and time table management, and examination management modules (pre and post exams, results, mark sheets). [pp. 7, 8, 9]
- Fees management module: configurable fee structures, collection, dues alerts, late fines, scholarships, waivers and refunds. [pp. 9, 10]
- Administration and establishment (HR) module, and payroll system covering leave, salary, arrears, pension contribution and appraisals. [p. 10]
- Finance and accounts, budgeting, and purchase and inventory management modules (vouchers, general ledger, procure to pay, stock). [p. 11]
- Hostel management, student feedback management, and faculty/employee and student self-service portals. [pp. 11, 12]
- Website re-design: link all ERP data to the institute website and upgrade it from time to time. [p. 12]
- Maintain the cloud based ERP under a contract for three years, priced as annual subscription charges. [p. 16]
