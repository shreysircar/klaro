## Overview

Development Partnership Administration-II, Ministry of External Affairs, Government of India, invites two-cover online bids from eligible institutes to design and deliver short-term Basic IT training for foreign participants under the ITEC programme. RFP No. DPA-II/551/05/2016/BasicIT, dated 20.12.2018. Selection is for three years, extendable by one year at each instance by mutual agreement. [pp. 1, 5]

## Issuing organization

Ministry of External Affairs, Government of India; Development Partnership Administration-II; RFP No. DPA-II/551/05/2016/BasicIT; Section Officer (ITEC-I), Room No. 2079, 'B' Wing, Jawaharlal Nehru Bhawan, New Delhi-110011, Tel: 011-49018130 [pp. 1, 5]

## Scope

- Design and deliver short-term Basic IT training programmes for foreign participants under ITEC; course duration from one week to up to 12 weeks, typically 30 participants per course. [p. 5]
- Intended allotment: up to 1200 man-weeks of Basic IT training per institute per year; actual participants attending may vary. [p. 16]
- Each programme must be customised in consultation with MEA; MEA may modify training areas, number of programmes and number of participants. [p. 5]
- Receive selected participants at the airport, provide lodging and boarding during training, impart training, arrange study tour (if part of the programme) and see participants off at the airport. [p. 13]
- Provide emergency support including medical, and ensure continental food at subsidized rates; food payment is settled between participant and institute. [p. 13]
- Disburse the participants' living allowance on behalf of MEA on a weekly or fortnightly basis, with the first instalment on the day of arrival. [p. 15]
- Arrange single-occupancy hostel or hotel accommodation with air conditioning, attached toilets, TV, bedding and toiletries, in-room tea/coffee maker, locker, security and recreational facilities. [p. 16]
- Submit reports on the ITEC online portal as well as offline. [p. 13]
