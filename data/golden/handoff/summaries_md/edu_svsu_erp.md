## Overview

Shri Vishwakarma Skill University (SVSU), Gurugram, Haryana, invites e-tenders for design, development, configuration and implementation of a cloud based Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) system. Implementation is estimated at 1 Year from issuance of work order, followed by AMC support (operation and maintenance) for 5 Years. Tender Reference Number: SVSU/2020/ITCELL/RFP001. [pp. 1, 2]

## Issuing organization

Shri Vishwakarma Skill University (SVSU), Gurugram, Haryana; Tender Reference Number SVSU/2020/ITCELL/RFP001; contact office: Registrar, Plot 147, Sector 44, Gurugram, registrar@svsu.ac.in; Dr. Ravinder Kumar [pp. 1, 3]

## Scope

- Design, develop, deploy (cloud hosting for five years) and maintain the ERP system, and customize and enhance module functionality as per SVSU requirements during the contract period. [p. 12]
- Provision the cloud infrastructure for hosting, plus a dashboard with DNS and DNS hosting; operate and maintain the application and cloud infrastructure. [p. 12]
- Support up to 30,000 users /year (Approx.). [p. 12]
- Integrated ERP of 14 modules, including Administration, Student Management, Examination and Skill Assessment, Accounting & Finance, Library, Estate, IQAC, International Collaboration, Skill School, Training and Placement, Affiliation Portal, Students Activity, Web/Mobile and Virtual Classroom. [pp. 12, 13]
- Develop the university website, the feeder school website and the ERP web interface, plus a mobile application giving access to selected ERP modules and employee records. [pp. 16, 17]
- Develop a virtual classroom, meeting and webinar platform with audio/video conferencing, recording, whiteboard and capacity of up to 500 participants. [p. 17]
- Integrate with any LMS or other SVSU module, and include SMS, e-mail and payment integration across all modules, with drill down dashboards and MIS reports. [p. 17]
- Train SVSU users for two weeks (i.e. 40 hrs. of training / week) per batch and provide user manuals for students, faculty and administrative staff. [pp. 38, 39]
