## Overview

Santa Fe County's Growth Management Department seeks multiple on-call firms with fire safety and engineering expertise to provide technical review of Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS), including lithium-ion and other battery storage technologies, under RFP No. 2026-0385-GM/JP. Consultants review development applications and BESS submittals for compliance with County ordinances, IFC, IWUIC, NFPA 855 and the SLDC. The contract is scheduled to begin September, 2026 with an initial term of four years. [pp. 1, 5, 21]

## Issuing organization

Santa Fe County; Growth Management Department and Purchasing Division; RFP No. 2026-0385-GM/JP; contact John Anthony Peperas, Procurement Specialist Senior, 102 Grant Avenue, First Floor, Santa Fe, NM 87501, (505) 986-6218, jpeperas@santafecountynm.gov [pp. 1, 7, 10]

## Scope

- Review BESS proposals for compliance with Santa Fe County Ordinances 2023-06 and 2023-09, the International Fire Code, IWUIC, NFPA 855 and the Sustainable Land Development Code. [p. 5]
- Review Conditional Use Permit (CUP) applications for completeness, including applicable studies, research to substantiate staff's recommendation, and the proposed BESS location, features and preliminary site plan. [p. 5]
- Attend at least two public hearings to provide expert testimony on proposed project design, technology and safety features. [p. 6]
- Review BESS submittals and required re-submittals under IFC, NFPA 855 and other codes for compliance, accuracy and overall safety. [p. 6]
- Submittal review covers fire and explosion testing data, the Hazard Mitigation Plan, NFPA 68 and 69 calculations or modeling, and other NFPA 855 test data. [p. 6]
- Submittal review also covers Emergency Operations and Response Plans, the Pre-Incident Plan, smoke and gas plume analysis, and the commissioning plan and testing reports. [p. 6]
- Assess fire protection measures for lithium-ion and other storage systems, and thermal runaway causes and their mitigation. [p. 6]
- Review final construction drawings for each proposed development and report all compliance variances or issues to Growth Management and County Fire Compliance Officers. [p. 5]
