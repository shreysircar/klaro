## Overview

The Centre for Research and Training in Informatics (CRTI), Department of Information Technology, Gangtok, Sikkim, seeks a firm to develop and implement the Sikkim Mobile Service Delivery App, an Android and iOS app for citizen services (Tender Reference 03/CRTI/DIT/2020, dated 17/02/2020). Go-live is due at T + 36 weeks from award of work, followed by operation of the system for 1 year and maintenance and support for three (3) years. [pp. 1, 7, 10]

- Develop and implement the Sikkim Mobile Service Delivery App, a one-stop mobile app for Government to Citizen services, in Android and iOS versions. [pp. 6, 10]
