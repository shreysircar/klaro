## Overview

Alameda County Community Development Agency (Economic & Civic Development Department) seeks economic development consulting for two scopes: ECD, for unincorporated communities, and the Surplus Property Authority (SPA), for property disposition. It intends a 3-year contract with an option to renew for 2 years; the estimated budget is $300,000 (2-$150,000 agreements). [pp. 1, 4]

## Issuing organization

## Scope

- ECD: economic development services for the unincorporated communities of Castro Valley, San Lorenzo, Ashland, Cherryland, Sunol, and the rural winegrowing region of unincorporated East County. [p. 4]
- ECD services include retail market demand and key development project analyses, innovative post-redevelopment financing approaches, support for food-related businesses, and implementation of a new economic development strategic plan. [p. 4]
- ECD: assist with reuse of former Redevelopment Agency properties, including the historic Lorenzo Theater and 20095 Mission Boulevard; may include review of proformas for affordable housing and other developments. [p. 4]
- SPA: assist with property disposition, including market analysis, Surplus Land Act compliance, preparing and reviewing requests for proposals, and supporting exclusive negotiation agreements and other disposition documents. [p. 4]
- Proposers may respond to ECD only, SPA only, or both; the RFP may result in one or multiple proposers with separate agreements for ECD and SPA. [p. 4]
- Selected proposer must create a scope and budget for specific projects as requested and attend regularly scheduled meetings set by CDA staff, where projects are determined. [p. 5]
- Deliverables that may arise: presentations, meetings with commissions and stakeholders, and feasibility analyses of various projects. [p. 5]
