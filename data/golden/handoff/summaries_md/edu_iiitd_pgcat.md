## Scope

- Provide infrastructure (human resources and machines) for the PGCAT-IIITD computer-based MCQ test for M.Tech. CSE, CSE-Research and PhD CSE on Sunday, May 3rd 2026; one shift, approx 2000 candidates in total; test duration 02 hours. [pp. 7, 8]
