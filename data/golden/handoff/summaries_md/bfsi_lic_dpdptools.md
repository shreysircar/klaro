## Overview

Life Insurance Corporation of India (LIC), Central Office, Mumbai, invites online two-bid tenders for procurement and implementation of tools for the DPDP Act, 2023 and DPDP Rules, 2025, covering 12 modules such as consent management, data discovery and breach management, for a period of five (5) years. Ref: LIC-CO/ERM/CSD/RFP/2026-27/DPDPA_Tools Dated: 13/07/2026. [pp. 1, 9, 11]

## Scope

- Supply, implement and support a DPDP compliance solution of 12 modules: Data Privacy Notice Management, Consent Management Platform, Cookie Consent Management, Data Discovery & Mapping, Data Principal Rights Management (including Grievance Redressal), DPIA, ROPA, Personal Data Breach Management, Third Party Vendor Risk Management, Compliance Reporting & Dashboards, Workflow Management and a DPDP Program Management portal. List is not exhaustive. [pp. 11, 27]
- Six phases with deliverables for each module: Planning & Designing workshop, Implementation, User acceptance testing, Movement to production, Training and knowledge transfer, and Post implementation support. The activity list is indicative, not exhaustive. [p. 27]
- Supply and install software and hardware (if any) for DC and DR in HA mode, deployed in pre-production, production and DR environments, with all licences registered in LIC's name. [pp. 28, 35]
- Integrate with LIC applications and systems including SIEM, PAM, Active Directory/LDAP, SMS and email gateways and a centralized identity repository; the solution should work with SOAR for incident handling. [pp. 28, 35, 38]
- Bidder must engage OEMs for plan, design and implementation; the OEM holds overall implementation responsibility, certifies the architecture and audits deployment at the end of implementation and once every year during the contract period. [pp. 28, 29, 33]
- After go-live, manage and monitor the solution end to end for the five-year term, with level-1, level-2 and level-3 support, root cause analysis, VAPT remediation, DR drill support and a 24/7/365 support contact centre. [pp. 35, 37]
- Training at LIC premises in Mumbai: hands-on training before and after implementation, certification-level training for three participants at no additional cost, and training twice every year for each module till end of contract. [pp. 31, 36]
- Deploy a total of 24 dedicated onsite resources for the contract duration, including an onsite Project Manager, on LIC premises in Mumbai. [pp. 43, 44, 45]
