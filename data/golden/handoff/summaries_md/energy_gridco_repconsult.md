## Overview

GRIDCO Limited, a Government of Odisha undertaking, invites e-tenders on a QCBS, single stage two-bid basis to appoint a consultancy firm on retainership to support it, as Nodal Agency, in implementing the Odisha Renewable Energy Policy, 2022. The contract is for 36 months, extendable by 24 months, with a project management team based in Bhubaneswar. Tender notice no. HR/03/2022-23. [pp. 1, 2, 4, 26]

## Issuing organization

GRIDCO Limited (wholly owned undertaking of Government of Odisha); Janpath, Bhoinagar, Bhubaneswar-751022, Odisha; e-Tender Notice No. HR/03/2022-23 dated 04.03.2023; contact Ms. Rosaline Biswal, DGM (HRD) [pp. 1, 2, 25]

## Scope

- Project identification and approval: resource potential assessment for all RE technologies, a registry and pipeline of RE projects, and papers for Single Window Committee approvals. [p. 31]
- Bid process management: prepare standard bid documents for Pump Storage Hydro, Large Hydro and Small hydro, Floating Solar, and Solar and Wind, and assist the end-to-end bid process. [pp. 31, 32]
- Project facilitation: act as single window facilitator, help developers obtain statutory clearances, prepare a directory of government lands, and support GRIDCO as Secretariat for the Single Window Committee and the Policy Advocacy and Vision Group. [p. 32]
- Technical support: monitor pre-developmental work of identified projects and give project development assistance and technical advice for RE projects in the State. [p. 33]
- Project monitoring: build a project management framework, work breakdown structures, milestones, risk registers, and status reports at project and portfolio level. [p. 33]
- Policy and regulatory support: study policy impact, track new Regulations, support OERC and draft Rules, follow up with MoP, MNRE and Energy Department, and prepare monthly, quarterly and yearly RPO compliance reports. [pp. 33, 34]
- Support to aggregate RE demand from industries: reach out to industries, assist RE procurement planning, and support bid process management for RE procurement and signing of PPA. [p. 34]
- Single window coordination: coordinate with all state agencies for clearances and approval of RE projects, and support department and developers till award of projects. [p. 34]
