## Overview

Jharkhand Renewable Energy Development Agency (JREDA), Government of Jharkhand, invites bids under NIB 12/JREDA/SPV/LOHARDAGA/OFFGRID/23-24 for design, engineering, supply, installation, testing and commissioning of solar PV mini/micro grids and solar stand-alone systems with distribution network, control room and household connections, plus 5 years Operation and Comprehensive Maintenance (CMC), on turnkey basis in 08 villages of Lohardaga District. - Six solar mini/micro grids in Block Peshrar, each with distribution network, control room and household connections: Doodhpatan-1 4.5 kWp, Doodhpatan-2 Bhagat Tola 3.6 kWp, Ganeshpur 16.5 kWp, Chari 10.5 kWp, Manhepaat 16.5 kWp, Hurmur 13.5 kWp. [pp. 5, 74]

- Power distribution network: in every selected household minimum three (3) light points with 3 LED lamps of 9W/10W/11watt, one socket point (60W) and one socket point (100 W), 193 W up to 200 W in total; 8 meters poles; one 18 W LED street light per five households. [pp. 47, 48]

## Key requirements

- Valid test reports approved by MNRE/BIS must be submitted for solar module, PCU, battery and solar home light system; all are mandatory for technical eligibility. Bidder must also undertake it can supply the offered quantity within the schedule. [p. 10]
- Modules must conform to IEC 61215/IS14286 and IEC 61730 Part 1 and 2; output must be warranted not less than 90% at the end of ten (10) years and 80% at the end of twenty five (25) years; efficiency above 15% desirable. [p. 30]
- Each SPV module must carry an RFID tag placed inside the module laminate holding manufacturer, cell manufacturer, manufacture dates, country of origin, I-V curve, serial number and IEC certificate details. [p. 31]
- A three-phase hybrid PCU must be used with each power plant, but another clause allows a single-phase inverter below 10kW; anti-islanding must conform to IEEE 1547/UL 1741/IEC 62116 or equivalent BIS standard. [pp. 37, 39]
- Remote monitoring: site data must be available on a web server for monitoring and control over GPRS (4G/5G enabled); bidder must provide the SIM card and bear its monthly charge. [pp. 34, 39]
- Battery bank must be tubular VRLA/GEL 2 volt cells conforming to the MNRE programme; automotive batteries will not be accepted; depth of discharge must not be more than 80%. [p. 41]
- Bidder must install a Time-of-Day (ToD) meter at each site recording 15-minute block-wise generation and supply, storing 15-minute data for 40-45 days and day-wise data for 6 months. [p. 41]
- Mounting structure must withstand minimum wind speed of 150 km/ hour (hot dip galvanized MS permitted, aluminium also allowed); earth resistance must not be more than 5 ohms; lightning protection as per IEC 62305. [pp. 43, 44]
- Stand-alone system (solar power pack): minimum 200 Wp module, 12.8 Volts 80 Ahr lithium ferro phosphate battery only with cycle life more than 2000 cycles, MPPT charge controller, 5 W-LED luminaires of 7.0 Watts each, one DC pedestal fan of 20Watts; indigenous modules preferred. [pp. 49, 50]
- Items must pass pre-dispatch inspection by JREDA at the manufacturing site (sample per BIS norms or minimum @ 2% of quantity offered); site must have NABL-conforming testing facilities; no dispatch without Dispatch Clearance; failure leads to work order cancellation and forfeiture of security deposit. [p. 25]
