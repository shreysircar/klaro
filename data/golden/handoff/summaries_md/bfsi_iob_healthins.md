## Overview

Indian Overseas Bank invites IRDAI-licensed Stand-Alone Health Insurance companies for a corporate agency tie-up to distribute health insurance products to its customers through branches and digital channels. The arrangement is initially for 3 years, renewable at the Bank's discretion for a further 3 years. Reference RFP/SAHI/003/2026-27, dated 11.05.2026. [pp. 1, 7]

## Issuing organization

Indian Overseas Bank; Marketing & Development Department, 763 Anna Salai, Central Office, Chennai-600002; Ref. RFP/SAHI/003/2026-27; queries: mdd@iob.bank.in, 044-28519639/ 044-71729204 [pp. 1, 4]

## Scope

- Corporate agency arrangement with one or more additional Stand-Alone Health Insurance companies to distribute individual and group health insurance products through branches and digital channels including internet banking and mobile banking. [p. 7]
- Customise insurance products for every segment of the Bank's customers, with premiums in tune with market rates and competitive. [p. 7]
- Support the Bank on sales, renewals, claims, customer complaint resolution, MIS, training, IT and manpower across the country. [p. 8]
- Provide day-to-day data on policies issued, renewals, lapses and claims, with real-time data exchange with the Bank, and give the Bank portal access through a User ID and password. [p. 8]
- Source business from all centres for the initial 01 year; permission for the subsequent 2 years may be extended by the Bank on satisfactory performance, subject to annual review. [p. 8]
- Provide MIS updates on status of customer complaints on a weekly/fortnightly/monthly basis. [p. 8]
- Settle claims without delay and within IRDAI timelines, run a grievance redressal procedure, issue renewal notices well before expiry, and issue policy documents and endorsements without delay. [p. 9]
- Annexure 16 information security scope applies if data is hosted on the successful bidder's own premises (data centre). [p. 40]

## Eligibility

- Must hold a valid IRDAI insurance licence, valid on the date of RFP submission; copy of original licence and current licence with proof of renewal required. [p. 10]
- Must be a government organization/PSU or a company registered under the Companies Act 1956 /2013 and with IRDAI, in existence for at least 5 years in standalone health insurance with business in India in the last 5 years. [p. 10]
- Must have at least 3 years of experience in Bancassurance and a corporate agency tie-up with at least 2 Scheduled Commercial Banks, preferably 1 Public Sector Bank; certificate from beneficiary bank required. [p. 10]
- Must have a minimum Claim Settlement Ratio of 90% in the last 2 financial years as on 31.03.2025 (FY24-25 and FY23-24). [p. 10]
- Must have a minimum Solvency Ratio of 150% as on 31.03.2025 (C.A. certificate basis). [p. 10]
- Must have minimum average Rs.5000 Crores underwritten premium (new business plus renewal) in the last 2 years. [p. 10]
- Must sign the Integrity Pact (Annexure-17), a preliminary qualification; in a joint venture all partners must sign. [pp. 19, 49]
- Must confirm in Annexure 15 that it is not blacklisted by any tie-up partner, PSU, PSB or private bank, has no pending cheating/fraud case, and has no IRDAI or other regulatory penalty or warning (details to be given if any). [p. 39]
